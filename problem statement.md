Cloud-based collaborative project management systems enable multiple users and organizations to share projects, tasks, and documents, but the concentration of shared resources and user activities introduces security risks such as unauthorized access, excessive privileges, insecure document sharing, account compromise, insider misuse, weak tenant isolation, and insufficient monitoring. Conventional access-control and logging mechanisms may provide basic protection but may not adequately identify abnormal user behaviour or assess the security risk associated with ongoing activities. Therefore, there is a need to investigate and develop a cloud-based collaborative project management platform that integrates fine-grained authorization, secure document sharing, comprehensive audit logging, behavioural anomaly detection, and risk scoring to improve the security monitoring and protection of collaborative cloud environments
